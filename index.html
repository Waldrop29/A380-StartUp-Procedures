A380 Start Up Checklist

  • 1. Pre-flight Walkaround

    • Front Right section

      • Inspect nose gear for low pressure and condition
      • Inspect taxi lights
      • Inspect FO Probes and Ports for leaks and damage
      • Inspect FO Windscreen for cracks and damage

      Right Wing and Undercarriage

      • Inspect landing lights for damage and proper operation
      • Inspect LE devices
      • Inspect trailing edge devices for damage
      • Inspect position lights
      • Inspect navigation lights
      • Inspect strobe lights
      • Inspect flaps
      • Inspect slats
      • Inspect engine nacelles for damage
      • Inspect engine cowls for damage
      • Inspect thrust reversers and turbofan blades for damage
      • Inspect lower beacon lights and antenna for damage
      • Inspect right main landing gear for low pressure and condition
      • Inspect left main landing gear for low pressure and condition

      Rear section

      • Inspect tail cone for damage
      • Inspect vertical stabilizer for damage
      • Inspect horizontal stabilizer for damage
      • Inspect rudder for damage
      • Inspect elevators for damage
      • Inspect rear beacon lights and antenna for damage
      • Inspect APU exhaust for damage

      Left Wing

      • Inspect trailing edge devices, slats, and flaps for damage
      • Inspect strobe, nav, and position lights for damage and proper operation
      • Inspect LE devices for damage
      • Inspect turbofan blades and thrust reversers for damage
      • Inspect engine nacelles for damage
      • Inspect engine cowls for damage
      • Inspect landing lights for damage and proper operation

      Front Left

      • Inspect CPTN probes and ports for blockades and damage
      • Press Shift+C to enter aircraft

    2. Electrical & APU Startup

    • Click on the EFB to start it up
    • Press BTY 1, 2, ESS BTY, and APU BTY switches
    • Press EXT PWR 2, 3, 1, and 4 in that order
    • Set NAV Lights to ON
    • Perform APU Fire Test
    • Turn APU Master Switch ON
    • Wait 3 seconds, then turn APU Starter ON
    • Verify APU AVAIL on System Display
    • Turn APU Bleed ON
    • Turn OFF EXT PWR 1, 2, 3, 4 and disconnect GPU ground cable via EFB

    3. Cockpit & ADIRS Alignment

    • Set Cockpit Packs to AUTO
    • Set ADIRS 1, 2, and 3 rotary switches to NAV mode
    • Set No Smoking signs to AUTO/ON
    • Set Seatbelts switch to ON

    4. FMS Route & Performance Setup

    • Open FMS on MFD and select INIT A
    • Input Departure, Arrival, Flight Number, and press ALIGN IRS
    • Select PERF/WEIGHT page and enter ZFW, Cargo, and Block Fuel
    • Select FPLN page and set SID, Waypoints, and Initial Cruise Altitude
    • Set Altitude on FCU panel and update local Altimeter (QNH)

    5. Engine Startup

    • Verify all doors show CLOSED/ARMED on SD
    • Turn Beacon Light to ON
    • Obtain Pushback & Start Clearance
    • Set ENG SELECTOR switch to START
    • Turn Engine 4 Switch ON (Monitor N2, Fuel Flow, EGT until AVAIL)
    • Turn Engine 1 Switch ON (Monitor until AVAIL)
    • Turn Engine 3 Switch ON (Monitor until AVAIL)
    • Turn Engine 2 Switch ON (Monitor until AVAIL)
    • Set ENG SELECTOR switch back to NORM
    • Turn APU Bleed and APU Master Switch OFF

    8. Taxi Preparation

    • Set Flaps to Takeoff position (Flaps 1 or 2)
    • Set Pitch Trim to takeoff percentage
    • Set Autobrake to MAX
    • Perform Flight Control check (Yoke full up/down/left/right, Rudder left/right)
    • Arm Spoilers
    • Turn Nose/Taxi Light to TAXI
    • Release Parking Brake and proceed to taxiway
